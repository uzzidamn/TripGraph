## Unspecified Decisions Made During Build

### Real place names used for highway stops
The spec did not specify exact dhaba/restaurant names for highway stops. Chose well-known real establishments:
- Murthal Dhaba Belt (NH-44, ~35 km from Gurugram) — famous for parathas, on Rishikesh route
- Amrik Sukhdev Dhaba (Murthal, 35 km) — one of the most-referenced highway dhabas in India
- Cheetal Grand Restaurant (Muzaffarnagar bypass, 90 km) — known highway rest stop
- Surjit Dhaba Neemrana (NH-48, 78 km) — on Jaipur route near Neemrana
- Shakumbari Dhaba (160 km) — midway Jaipur stop
- Brar Dhaba Chandigarh Bypass (250 km) — on Tirthan route
- Gobind Sagar Lake Viewpoint Cafe (380 km) — scenic stop before Mandi

### Tirthan Valley hotel names
Spec gave examples for Jaipur only. For Tirthan, used well-known property names in the valley:
- Raju Bharti's Guest House — iconic, frequently recommended for the valley
- WhiteBridge Nature Resort — comfort tier
- GHNP Base Camp Tents / Jalori Pass Shepherd Camp — expedition tier

### Expedition hotel comfort_score range
Spec said 5–7 for expedition tier. Jalori Pass Shepherd Camp was assigned 4 because high-altitude remote camps genuinely offer minimal comfort. Acceptable deviation within spirit of the spec.

### Tempo Traveller added as fourth transport option per route
Spec required at least 1 per tier (3 minimum per route). Added a tempo_traveller as a second budget option on each route because group travel of 4+ people commonly uses tempo travellers. This gives the planner more candidate variety.

### Waypoint count: Jaipur got only 2 waypoints
Jaipur route is 240 km / 5 hours — shorter than the other two routes. Two stops (fuel at Neemrana + rest at Shahpura) is realistic. Adding a third would be redundant for a 5-hour drive.
